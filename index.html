Grammar Topic Overview

1. Gerunds (-ing)

Used as subjects, after specific verbs (enjoy, avoid, suggest), prepositions, and phrasal verbs.

2. To-Infinitives

Used after specific verbs (decide, hope, afford), adjectives, and to express purpose.

3. Meaning Changes

Verbs like remember, stop, try, regret, and mean change their meaning significantly when followed by gerunds vs. infinitives.

4. Bare Infinitives

Used after modal verbs, causative verbs (make, let), and perception verbs (see, hear).
